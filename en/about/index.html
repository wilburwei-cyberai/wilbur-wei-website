WILBUR WEI / ABOUT

Wilbur Wei / Te-En Wei

Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Yuan Ze University. AI security speaker and consultant. Publications appear under Te-En Wei or 魏得恩.

PERSPECTIVE

Research, teaching and practice.
One connected perspective.

AI security concerns more than models: who can access data, who can invoke tools and how an organisation makes decisions. These questions connect my research, teaching and consulting.

At Yuan Ze University, I research AI security and zero trust, collaborate with industry on detection and deception, and turn technical work into seminars, corporate courses and lab materials.

EXPERIENCE

Experience across different settings

Academic and industry research

Assistant Professor at Yuan Ze University, researching AI agent security, zero trust, APTs and AD risk, with research collaborations involving Billows Tech. and Zyell Solutions.

Audit and governance experience

Experience as a cybersecurity audit committee member for the Ministry of Health and Welfare, informing governance and training work.

Industry engagement and teaching

Industry speaking experience, including as honorary chair of TWSMEISA, and teaching or speaking engagements with TCFST, government training programmes and 中華電信學院.
