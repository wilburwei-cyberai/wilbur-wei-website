PROCESS

Start with a clear scope.

Project-based or ongoing advice can be discussed according to the problem and the team’s needs.

  1. Context and scope

    Agree on the business context, decision-makers, deployment stage and outcomes.

  2. Review and assessment

    Review architecture, data flows, permissions and existing controls within the agreed scope.

  3. Recommendations and priorities

    Connect risks to controls and discuss feasibility, responsibilities and priorities.

  4. Deliverables and follow-through

    Deliver the agreed analysis and recommendations, with training or follow-up advice where appropriate.

QUESTIONS

Before we work together

Can we talk before deploying AI?

Yes. Intended use cases, data categories and tool permissions provide a basis for bringing security requirements into selection and planning.

What are the consulting deliverables?

Deliverables may include a risk inventory, control recommendations, prioritised improvements, architecture review notes or training. Scope, required inputs and acceptance criteria are agreed before work begins.

How does an engagement start?

Start with a non-confidential outline of the business context, deployment stage, current problem and desired outcome. We can then discuss scope, timing and the engagement format.
