WILBUR WEI / TEACHING

Courseware people can understand—and use.

Connect concepts, examples and practice through audience needs, source quality and the learning environment.

  1. Verify sources before drafting

    Check cases and figures against sources, preserving dates, conditions and context. Distinguish company claims, experimental findings and interpretation.

  2. Test from the learner’s environment

    Check accounts, tool versions, dependencies and steps for the course environment, so exercises are usable beyond the instructor’s machine.

  3. Connect judgement to scenarios

    Use data-handling, rewriting and cross-functional scenarios that ask learners to explain their decisions. Technical courses use synthetic data or teaching environments.

  4. AI-assisted work, human editorial responsibility

    Use AI to assist organisation and cross-checking, with the instructor responsible for final content, sources and teaching decisions.
