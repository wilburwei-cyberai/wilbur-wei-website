ABOUT / PERSPECTIVE

An AI security consultant
across academia,
government and industry

People often ask: are you a professor, a consultant or a government committee member? My answer is all three. Working across these roles gives me a fuller view of the threat landscape.

When AI agents begin executing tasks autonomously and generative AI enters core business systems, the attack surface changes fundamentally. As an AI security consultant and speaker, I study these emerging threats and help organisations think through their defensive architecture before deploying the technology.

These three roles have taught me the same lesson: cybersecurity in the AI era is as much about management decisions and organisational culture as it is about technology.

2025 / CISC

STIE-ZTA

Zero-trust architecture requires continuous verification of every subject that requests access. The practical difficulty is that enterprises already run many security tools — intrusion detection, firewalls, endpoint protection — each producing its own data, with no unified way to combine them into a judgement about whether a subject can be trusted. STIE-ZTA is a policy-based, self-adaptive trust inference framework that aggregates and analyses this heterogeneous data to evaluate each subject's behavioural trust level continuously, so that organisations can make timelier and better-grounded access decisions while getting more out of the infrastructure they already have.
